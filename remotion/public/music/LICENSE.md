# Music library

Tracks a `cut.json` can name in its `music` field, by file name:

```json
{ "music": "digital-clouds.mp3" }
```

The files are **versioned with the toolkit**, so a track only belongs here if its licence allows
redistribution and commercial use. Adding one means adding its line below — **a track without a
licence line does not ship**.

| File | Track | Artist | Source | Licence | Fetched |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `digital-clouds.mp3` | Digital Clouds | Alejandro Magaña | https://mixkit.co/free-stock-music/ | Mixkit Stock Music Free License — no attribution required, commercial use, YouTube and social allowed | 2026-09-22 |

The film plays one track at 55 % volume, faded in over 0.7 s and out over the last 2 s, looped if
it is shorter than the render and trimmed to it. No narration, no captions.
